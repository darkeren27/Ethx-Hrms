2. Access Control Principles & Boundary Enforcement

  • Principle of Least Privilege: Employees, interns, and contract personnel can only access their own dossier, shift ledger, leave applications, and assigned assets. Cross-user ID manipulation (IDOR) is strictly rejected at the service and query layer.
  • Designated Approver Isolation: Niky Sharma (ETHX-005) is the authorized Main HR operator for company attendance and leave approvals. Madhavi Singh (ETHX-006) is IT HR and does not inherit administrative approval rights.
  • Self-Approval Prohibition & Escalation: Niky Sharma cannot approve her own leave applications. Her requests automatically route to Company Director Ram Chaturvedi (ETHX-001) for executive sign-off.
  • Intern Policy Bounds: 8 Unpaid Interns (ETHX-014 to ETHX-021) operate under a strict 1 July 2026 – 30 September 2026 schedule with an unpaid-leave-only policy. The end date will not trigger automated account deletion, fabricated salary deductions, or unauthorized status alterations.

4. Continuous Integration Pipeline Specification

Pipeline Triggers & Security Baseline
  • Triggers: push to main / master, pull_request to default branch, and manual workflow_dispatch.
  • Permissions: contents: read (Least privilege principle; tokens cannot write to repository).
  • Concurrency: Grouped by ref with cancel-in-progress: true to avoid redundant resource consumption.
  • Timeout: 15 minutes strict threshold to eliminate hung jobs.

Stage Commands Executed Security & Quality Objectives
